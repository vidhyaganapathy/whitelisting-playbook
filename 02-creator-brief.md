# 02 · Creator brief (built for paid)

Organic content is judged on reach and engagement. Whitelisted content is judged on **leads or installs at a target cost**. The brief has to reflect that.

Copy the template below for each creator.

---

## Template

### 1. The goal
- **Campaign objective:** [Leads / App installs / Sales / Traffic]
- **What a win looks like:** [e.g. qualified leads at or below target CPL]
- **Where the ad sends people:** [Landing page / app store / lead form]

### 2. The audience
- **Who we're trying to reach:** [role, life stage, problem they have]
- **What they believe now:** [the objection or habit we need to shift]
- **Why this creator:** [audience fit, tone, credibility on the topic]

### 3. The message
- **One thing to land:** [single benefit, in plain words]
- **Proof:** [demo, result, before/after, personal story]
- **Offer:** [free trial, download, discount, booking]
- **CTA (must match the destination):** [e.g. "Download free", "Book a call"]

### 4. Must-haves
- [ ] **Hook in the first 2 seconds:** say or show the problem straight away
- [ ] **Product or app visible on screen** within the first few seconds
- [ ] **Captions or on-screen text** (most people watch muted)
- [ ] **Vertical 9:16**, safe zones clear of UI overlays
- [ ] **Clear CTA** at the end, spoken and on screen

### 5. Hooks to film
Ask for **3 alternative openings** on the same body, so you can test hooks without new shoots.
1. [Problem hook: "If you're still…"]
2. [Result hook: "I did X and…"]
3. [Curiosity hook: "Nobody tells you…"]

### 6. Deliverables
| Deliverable | Format | Length | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main video | 9:16 Reel | 15–30s | [date] |
| Hook variations | 9:16 | first 3s × 3 | [date] |
| Raw footage | unwatermarked | n/a | [date] |

### 7. Don'ts
- No claims we can't back up
- No competitor mentions
- No music you don't have rights to use in ads
- [Brand-specific don'ts]

### 8. Rights recap
Usage window, platforms, edits and permission type, as agreed in [`01-rights-checklist.md`](./01-rights-checklist.md).

---

## Why it's built this way

- **Hooks are the biggest lever.** Three openings on one body gives you a hook test for the price of one video.
- **The CTA has to match the destination.** "Link in bio" doesn't work in an ad.
- **Raw files unlock iteration.** You can recut a winner instead of re-briefing.
