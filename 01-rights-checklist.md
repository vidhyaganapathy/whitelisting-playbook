# 01 · Rights checklist

Agree every item **in writing, before the brief goes out.** Adding paid usage after content is made usually means renegotiating the fee.

---

## Usage

- [ ] **Paid usage included?** Organic posting and paid usage are separate rights.
- [ ] **Usage window:** start and end date for paid usage (e.g. 30, 60 or 90 days)
- [ ] **Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, or both (and any others)
- [ ] **Placements:** Feed, Stories, Reels
- [ ] **Geography:** which markets the ads can run in
- [ ] **Extension option:** price and process to extend the window if the ad performs

## Permissions

- [ ] **Permission type:**
  - **Content-level:** one specific post (shared via a partnership ad code or the creator's boost setting)
  - **Account-level:** lets you promote their content over the partnership without a new code each time
- [ ] **Who grants it and when:** creator confirms they'll grant permission on posting day
- [ ] **Creator stays public:** the post must not be deleted, archived or made private during the window

## Creative

- [ ] **Edits allowed:** can you trim, add captions, change the first frame, add a CTA overlay?
- [ ] **Raw files:** will the creator send unwatermarked raw footage for cut-downs?
- [ ] **Number of hooks:** how many alternative opening lines or first shots they'll film
- [ ] **Caption and copy:** can you write the ad copy, or must it stay in the creator's words?
- [ ] **Approval:** who approves the final ad before it goes live

## Commercials

- [ ] **Fee:** content fee and paid-usage fee, itemised separately
- [ ] **Payment terms:** when and how the creator is paid
- [ ] **Exclusivity:** any category exclusivity, and for how long
- [ ] **Disclosure:** the paid partnership label stays on, as required by platform rules and local advertising law

## Comments and brand safety

- [ ] **Comment moderation:** who moderates comments on the ad, and what gets hidden
- [ ] **Brand safety:** creator's recent content reviewed for fit
- [ ] **Takedown:** what happens if either side needs to pause early
