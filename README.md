# Whitelisting Playbook

**Run paid ads through creator handles on Meta, end to end.**

Whitelisting means running paid ads from a creator's identity, not just your brand page. On Meta this is called **partnership ads** (previously "branded content ads"): the ad shows the creator's handle, and you control the targeting, budget and optimisation in your own Ads Manager.

This is the system I use to plan, brief, set up and test whitelisted creator ads, from the first conversation with a creator to the scale-or-kill decision.

---

## Why whitelisting

| Brand ad | Whitelisted creator ad |
|---|---|
| Comes from a brand page people don't follow | Comes from a person people already trust |
| Reads as an ad | Reads as a recommendation |
| Limited to brand creative | Unlocks creator-native hooks, formats and voice |
| Creative fatigue hits fast | Adds a new creative source for every creator you sign |

**When it's worth it:** you already have (or can brief) creator content, the offer is clear, and you have budget to test at least 3 creators properly.

**When it isn't:** the product needs heavy explanation, there's no conversion tracking in place, or the creator's audience doesn't match your buyer.

---

## The process

```
1. Rights first      →  01-rights-checklist.md
2. Brief for paid    →  02-creator-brief.md
3. Permissions + setup →  03-setup-sop.md
4. Test and decide   →  04-test-matrix.md
```

1. **Rights first.** Agree paid usage, duration, platforms, edits and fees *before* anything is filmed. Adding whitelisting after the fact usually means renegotiating.
2. **Brief for paid, not just organic.** The content has to work as an ad: a hook in the first seconds, a clear offer and a CTA that matches the destination.
3. **Permissions and setup.** Get the right permission type (one post or account-level), then build the ad in Ads Manager with the creator as the identity.
4. **Test and decide.** Run creators against each other and against brand creative, with kill rules set before launch.

## Principles

- **Rights before content.** No paid usage without written terms.
- **Creators are a paid channel.** Brief every piece for ads from day one.
- **Judge on qualified leads, not just CPL.** A cheap lead from the wrong audience is waste.
- **Match geography to the creator's audience.** The trust signal is strongest where their followers are.
- **Respect the end date.** A working ad code is never a reason to run past the agreed usage window.

---

## Mistakes I avoid

- Asking for whitelisting after the content is already posted
- Getting a one-post permission when you'll want to promote several pieces
- Briefing for organic reach, then expecting it to convert as an ad
- Testing one creator and calling the channel "not working"
- Letting ads run after the usage window ends
- Sending traffic to a page that doesn't match what the creator promised
